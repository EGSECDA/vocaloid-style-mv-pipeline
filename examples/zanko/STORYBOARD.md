# 残光 — Storyboard & Scene Specs

**Logline.** After the sun has gone, a girl (灯 Akari, original V家-style singer) holds on to the last remaining light, 残光. The sky cracks like glass. She falls into the night, keeps a small flame alive, gathers the pieces of her shattered heart, and at the end of the darkness walks a road made of her own tears toward the dawn: 「暗闇の果てで あなたに会いたい」.

**Colour script.** Intro black + one ember → P1 magic hour (verse 1a) → P2 night-blue with amber (verse 1b) → P3 alarm / abyss (pre-chorus) → P1 on fire (chorus 1) → 3D dusk flythrough → night + rain (verse 2) → P4 blue-teal + red lamps (pre 2) → P1→P4 fireflies (chorus 2) → 3D night crossing → P5 faded memories (bridge) → black (break) → P1 maximum (last chorus) → **dawn** (climax) → title reprise in dawn palette → hard cut → cyan afterimage of 残光.

**Master shapes.** Circle = light/memory (sun, ember, lamp, 。, iris, firefly, heart glow). Crack/shard = the broken sky and heart. Vermilion thread = her ribbon, the red line that runs through the whole film.

## Special scene contracts (args)
- `ember` `{mode:'breathe'|'ignite'|'collapse', stops:[[t0,t1]...]}`: centre ember, rings, sparks, micro-stop blackouts, HUD typing.
- `titleRiff` `{variant:'intro'|'outro', recap:[{bg,char}...] (outro)}`: title motion graphics locked to beats/riff accents.
- `afterimage` `{text:'残光', sub:'afterglow'}`
- `converge` `{}`: motifs spiral into the centre → white.
- `kanjiSlam` `{glyphs:['救','って','救','って'], char:'assets/char/profile_sing.png', charX, bg, fire:false}`: per-beat typographic slam with a character.

- `fall` `{}`: falling through darkness with upward streaks; time freeze on stops.
